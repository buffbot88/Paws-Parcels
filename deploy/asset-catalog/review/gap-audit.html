Level-20 Launch Readiness Audit

CLOVERVILLAGE 1–20 · CONTENT IN PROGRESS

Tiered launch: the game ships in tiers — Launch 1 = CloverVillage (levels 1–20), Launch 2 = HappyValley (21–40), Launch 3 = VoidDesert (41–60). This audit covers ONLY the first launch (CloverVillage, levels 1–20). HappyValley and VoidDesert are cataloged but DEFERRED until their launch tier.

All 17 file categories across 8 packs are audited and classified. For the FIRST LAUNCH specifically: CloverVillage environment, NPCs, the three playable classes, and the undead loot icon set are production-ready. The launch blockers are concentrated: a native enemy roster, a regional boss, dungeon interactables, item/consumable icons, quest content, and zone UI/audio. HappyValley and VoidDesert are deferred to their launch tiers.
